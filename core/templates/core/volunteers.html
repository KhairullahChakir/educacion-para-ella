Volunteer Profiles

How Our Volunteers Make an Impact

Our volunteer community brings diverse expertise to support every dimension of a student's learning journey.

👩‍🏫
Teaching Team

Language Educators (English, Spanish, German)

Passionate language teachers who lead weekly conversational and grammatical sessions, guiding students step-by-step through CEFR levels A0 to C1.

  • Small group lessons (4-8 students per class)
  • Tailored homework review & speaking encouragement
💻
Tech Team

Software Developers & Tech Mentors

Engineers and digital creators teaching JavaScript, responsive HTML/CSS, computational thinking, and remote digital skills to foster financial independence.

  • Live code-alongs and practical project building
  • 1-on-1 code reviews and freelancing guidance
🧘‍♀️
Well-being Team

Mindfulness & Mental Health Facilitators

Psychologists, counselors, and trained facilitators providing grounding circles, emotional decompression, and trauma-informed wellness spaces.

  • Weekly guided mindfulness & somatic grounding
  • Safe peer validation and coping strategies
📖
Content Team

Curriculum Developers & Translators

Volunteers translating lesson guides into Dari and Pashto, formatting low-bandwidth downloadable PDFs, and curating open-source learning packages.

  • Dari & Pashto curriculum translation
  • Low-bandwidth study material preparation
