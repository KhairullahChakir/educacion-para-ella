Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak Dari or Pashto to volunteer as a teacher?

No. Our language classes (English, Spanish, German) are taught in immersion format. We also have bilingual coordinators who assist absolute beginner levels whenever needed.

What is the minimum time commitment?

Most volunteer teachers dedicate 2 to 4 hours per week (1–2 class sessions of 60 minutes each, plus brief preparation time).

How is digital safety maintained?

Student and teacher safety is our highest priority. All virtual meetings are password-protected, cameras are optional for students, and recording of live faces without consent is strictly prohibited.
