Living under severe educational and societal bans creates acute stress, uncertainty, and feelings of isolation. When young women are confined to their homes or navigating displacement in foreign countries, cognitive retention and learning require mental safety.

Our mindfulness sessions are structured specifically to address these challenges. Led by empathetic facilitators, these sessions provide grounding techniques, emotional regulation exercises, and a trusted peer community where students are heard without judgment.

What Our Circles Include

Gentle, structured practices designed to reduce anxiety and nurture personal growth.

Daily 20-min routines

Safe Sisterhood Circles

Moderated peer listening spaces where participants can share their feelings, validate each other's experiences, and build lifelong friendships.

Moderated & confidential
