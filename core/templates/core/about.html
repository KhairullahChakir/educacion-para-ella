About Educación para Ella

A volunteer-led online education and well-being initiative created to support Afghan girls and women who have been deprived of access to formal schooling.

Since the restrictions placed on girls' education in Afghanistan, millions of bright, ambitious students have been prevented from attending secondary schools and universities. Traditional paths to literacy, independence, and career development disappeared overnight.

Educación para Ella ("Education for Her") was founded in September 2025 as a direct, grassroots response. We cannot dismantle every physical barrier immediately, but we can ensure that an Afghan girl's dream of learning, speaking new languages, and coding never dies.

"An online classroom is more than a place for lessons. It is a connection to the world, a supportive sisterhood, a source of dignity, and proof that their future still matters."
