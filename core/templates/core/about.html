Our Story

About Educación para Ella

A volunteer-led online education and well-being initiative created to support Afghan girls and women who have been deprived of access to education.

Why We Exist

Every Girl Deserves the Opportunity to Learn

Since the Taliban's return to power in Afghanistan, girls and women have faced severe restrictions on education and participation in public life. For many Afghan girls, continuing their education through traditional institutions is no longer possible.

Educación para Ella was created as a response to this situation. We cannot remove every barrier that Afghan girls face, but we can help ensure that their desire to learn does not disappear.

An online classroom can become more than a place for lessons. It can become a connection to the outside world, a community, a source of confidence, and a reminder that students' ambitions and abilities still matter.

The Project Begins

Started in response to severe restrictions on education for Afghan girls. The project was created to provide a safe and accessible way to continue learning, develop new skills, and stay connected to an educational community.

More than 25 volunteers from around the world now contribute their time, knowledge, and skills. Teachers, educators, professionals, and people who believe strongly in the right to education.

Expanding Our Reach

Combining language education, digital and creative skills, programming, and psychological well-being — creating a supportive learning community reaching students in Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan, and Iraq.
